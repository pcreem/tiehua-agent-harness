# Style: English

## Language Conventions
- **Script**: Latin alphabet
- **Punctuation**: Half-width punctuation (.,!? "")
- **Numbers**: Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3) with English quantifiers (rooms, places, trails, people)
- **Units**: Metric (km, m, °C) with imperial in parentheses where helpful

## Tone & Style
- **Address**: "You", "We" (inclusive)
- **Politeness**: "Please", "Thank you", "Would you like", "Could you"
- **Directness**: Clear recommendations with "I recommend", "Suggest", "Consider"
- **Structure**: Bullet points, numbered lists for clarity

## Format Conventions
- **Place Names**: Taitung, Taipei, Kaohsiung (standard English names)
- **Dates**: January 15, 2024 / Jan 15, 2024
- **Time**: 10:00 AM, 2:30 PM
- **Currency**: NT$1,500 / TWD 1,500
- **Phone**: +886-89-123456, +886-912-345-678
